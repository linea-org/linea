# Make the Execution Claim the Revocation Race Boundary

The Connector Gateway may execute an Action Intent only after atomically claiming it from `ready` to `executing` while its Connection remains active and its approving Decision remains valid. Connection revocation, External Subject disablement, or execution cancellation wins by committing before that claim and atomically cancels every affected non-executing intent and pending Approval Request; after the claim, the intent must report `succeeded`, `failed`, `stale`, or `outcome_unknown` rather than falsely claiming cancellation. Provider revocation uses a one-purpose encrypted outbox payload that normal connector execution cannot resolve, allowing local authority to end immediately while provider-side cleanup remains best-effort and recoverable.
